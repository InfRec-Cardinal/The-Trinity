# Stage 1 - Fill a direction's gap with published tasks

A model-grown direction needs a train task or an independent published task to hold out as unseen. Interactive and static protocols are both eligible under the same split requirements. Keep the direction's capability boundary: do not merge unrelated tasks or invent a benchmark to satisfy the split. Current tasks and their source groups are provided in the kickoff.

Use different query routes to find published protocols whose objectives, permitted observations, action spaces, feedback and success conditions fit this direction. Exclude audio inputs under the shared task policy; text/code, image and video protocols are all retained. Investigate independently sourced engines, generators, scenes, data and question pools. A different website or repository name alone is not independent provenance.

Choose sources and candidates yourself, including public benchmarks, human competitions, forum task/result discussions and other traceable public material. No named benchmark or required task-family inventory is supplied. Follow and attribute original rules, resources and evaluation evidence.

Record candidates with save_task, origin search, a stable canonical protocolId and mechanismSourceUrl null. Multiple protocols may share one page; do not duplicate one protocol across aliases. All candidates go through specification, independent review and a library-wide provenance audit before assignment and splitting.

The host requires successful web search results and the minimum searches in the kickoff. Continue until query routes stop adding candidates or the actual budget is exhausted. finish_supplement must accurately report the outcome and stopReason saturated or budget_exhausted. Tool errors do not count as completed coverage. Self-proposed tasks belong to the later validation stage, not this first task library.

Repair rejected tool submissions and keep going until accepted.
