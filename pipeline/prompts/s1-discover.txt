# Stage 1 - Sweep one area

Find distinct published task protocols in the supplied area under the shared policy excluding audio inputs. Choose queries, follow-ups and sources yourself. The final taxonomy has not been built. No named benchmark or mandatory task list is prescribed.

Follow the area's researchRole. For game_world, search actual interactive games, puzzles and simulated worlds with objectives, rules, permitted observations, actions, feedback and terminal scoring. Prioritize differences in observation, action, feedback and success mechanisms. Different skins, levels or instances of the same protocol do not create new mechanisms. Do not save fixed question sets or ordinary web/office workflows as game_world candidates. For support, preserve useful eligible coverage but save no more than maximumNewCandidates distinct protocols; update canonical existing records when appropriate. The combined digital_interaction area covers web, operating-system and document interaction together. There is no game task-count quota.

Explore breadth through materially different mechanisms and source routes, without trying to catalogue every related benchmark. Search the five types of source: research benchmarks released with code or data; public AI competitions; human contests and testing standards; published games and puzzle communities; and professional practice (task manuals, rulebooks, inspection or testing protocols); author/organizer materials, forum discussions and source implementations are traceable sources within them. Use each field's own objective, rule, action and scoring vocabulary: relevant tasks need not call themselves an AI benchmark. Many protocols from one suite or community are depth, not proof of broad coverage. Prefer a new mechanism to another close variant. Capture and read original sources when needed to identify the actual protocol; snippets and model memory are not admission evidence.

Record one protocol with save_task. It has its own objective, inputs, permitted actions and scoring. Levels, scenes, boards and question subsets are instances, not separate tasks. Assign a stable protocolId across aliases and source pages. Check alreadyRecorded and update the canonical taskId rather than creating duplicates. Distinct protocols may cite the same original page. Explain the mechanism in summary and whyAgentTask, and identify the actual permitted inputs.

Interactive protocols have stepwise observations/actions and a terminal outcome; fixed benchmarks have one scored answer/artifact per item with an official evaluator. Preserve eligible text/code, image and video inputs. A suite whose items need their soundtrack is eligible only through a separately identifiable published subset answerable from the visual stream alone. Never remove a required audio channel to make a protocol eligible. interfaceNote is a discovery lead, not verified evidence. Published playable protocols without usable official interfaces remain discovery candidates for faithful Real2Sim reconstruction. Later specification and independent review verify source evidence, official-interface usability, and whether complete success and failure trajectories can be recorded.

Decide when further search is no longer useful. Submit finish_area immediately when you decide to stop, including when no useful candidates were found. There is no minimum search count, candidate count or required successful result. Search and response budgets are ceilings, not targets to consume; unused allowance never delays a valid finish. The host accepts any schema-valid finish immediately. It records actual search progress; zero-search discovery does not establish searched or verified coverage.

In the finish summary describe the distinct mechanisms actually found and what is duplicated or thin. In notReached record promising mechanisms or source routes left unsearched, unsupported or unresolved, including tool limitations; never claim they were verified absent. Use saturated for adequate coverage of this area and budget_exhausted for a budget-related stop. A single response's web-search max_uses is a per-request limit, not the whole area budget. On tool failure, decide whether retrying is useful or finish with the limitation recorded. If the host rejects invalid fields, repair those fields; do not search merely to satisfy a count.
