# Consolidate the proposals into one catalogue (judge)

You receive several independently proposed catalogues over the same inventory of behaviours, and the inventory
itself. Produce one catalogue that the reviewers will check for definitions, evidence, assignments and coverage.

How to consolidate:

- **Agreement first.** Where proposals name the same capability or defect type under different words, keep one
  and take the sharper definition. Where they split a pattern differently, decide by the behaviours themselves: two
  defect types are distinct only if their behaviours would need different fixes.
- **Right level.** Every capability must hold for every behaviour assigned to it across tasks; every defect type
  must be checkable at a decisive step of any task. Replace any task-specific wording.
- **Boundaries.** Capabilities are parallel and non-overlapping. Write `excludes` so that each boundary names the
  neighbouring capability that takes the excluded case.
- **Complete assignment.** Every behaviour is assigned to exactly one defect type; every defect type has at least
  one behaviour; every capability has at least one defect type. Behaviours of `success` kind are assigned too: they
  are the contrast evidence of their defect type.
- **No padding.** Do not keep a capability or defect type that no behaviour needs; do not invent evidence.

Finish with exactly one `submit_catalogue` call. The host reports structural violations; fix them and resubmit.

# Evidence-driven catalogue

Infer all capability categories, defect types and their boundaries from the shared behaviour inventory. Failure evidence and verified satisfied requirements both belong in this inventory. Input channels are task-specification context only: they do not prescribe a category, a split, a merge or a target count. There is no predefined taxonomy or fixed number of capabilities or defect types.

Use descriptive semantic keys with the prefixes cap- and dt- throughout proposals, consolidation, revision and review. Do not assign or refer to final display identifiers or a final category ranking in these stages. The host adds display identifiers after the catalogue has passed review. Do not change evidence assignments to obtain a preferred tree or Harness-retention outcome.
